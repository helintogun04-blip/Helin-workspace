# OFF HOURS — application preparation, v05

## Portföy başlığı
OFF HOURS — Editorial Newsletter Design | Sample Project

## English portfolio description
OFF HOURS is a fictional lifestyle publication exploring coffee, city life and small everyday pauses. This two-page sample demonstrates a coherent editorial system through a cover-led opening page, readable article columns, a structured sidebar and consistent typography. Helin selected and refined the creative direction using visual references. The imagery, copy and layout were produced with AI assistance. This is a self-initiated sample, not a commissioned client project; no audience or business results are claimed.

## Role
Creative direction and review; AI-assisted editorial concept development.

## Başvuru taslağı — henüz gönderme
Hi,

Your newsletter already has its stories and photographs; the design should bring them together with a clear reading order and a consistent community feel.

I have attached a two-page concept newsletter sample to show the visual direction and page structure I can help develop. It is a fictional sample with AI-assisted imagery, copy and layout, not a previous client newsletter. My related experience includes collaborating on Just Kawa's logo, packaging and label design in Canva.

For your newsletter, I would work with your supplied content and photos, agree on one representative page first, and carry the approved style through the remaining pages. The attached sample is intentionally moody; your own audience and preferred style would guide the actual design.

Before confirming the scope, could you clarify whether you need a digital PDF, print-ready files or an editable source, and whether the $200 budget covers one 8–10-page issue? Please also share your deadline and expected revision rounds.

Best,
Helin

## Teklif planı
- Geçmiş kamuya açık ilandaki bütçe: $200 sabit. Net kazanç değildir.
- Önerilen ilk kapsam: tek sayı, en fazla 10 sayfa, hazır metin/fotoğraf, dijital PDF, iki toplu revizyon turu; müşteriyle teyit edilmeden anlaşılmış sayılmaz.
- Tahmini takvim: tüm girdiler geldikten sonra ilk sayfa 2 iş günü, onaydan sonra kalan sayfalar 3–4 iş günü. Bu bir planlama tahmini; müşteri tarihi teyit edilmedi.
- Kaynak dosya PPTX: metin kutuları ve fotoğraf düzenlenebilir. Canva içe aktarma, iPad açılışı ve font eşleşmesi henüz test edilmedi; Canva/InDesign dosyası vaat etme.
- Baskı: mevcut örnek dijital RGB PDF; CMYK, taşma payı veya matbaa uygunluğu iddia etme.
